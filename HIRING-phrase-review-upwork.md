## ▓▓ RATING RUBRIC (also lives in Sheet tab 2) ▓▓

Score each phrase 1–5 on three axes:

- **Native** — idiomatic, what a native exec actually says, not textbook. 5 = *"Let me push back on that a little."* / 1 = *"I do not agree with your opinion."*
- **Natural (spoken)** — flows aloud; contractions, rhythm; not written/formal register.
- **Executive presence** — status & backbone, no hedging/watery filler. Strong = *"If you can move on price, I can move on timing."* / Weak = grammar fine but status collapses (hedge, question-intonation).

**Verdict = Weak** when any axis is low / the phrase would make an exec sound off. Add a one-line *why*. Respect each phrase's **Zone** (Warm / Backbone / Weight) — that's the intended register; judge within it. **No rewriting in Phase 1.**

## ▓▓ INTERNAL — how to grade the test ▓▓

- Compare each candidate rewrite against **our own AI rewrite** of the same phrase (`phrase-replacements-final.json`, 340 replacements) + a blind native read. Hire only if they **demonstrably out-phrase the machine**.
- Watch for AI tells: uniform scores, generic "why" lines, suspiciously fast turnaround, ChatGPT-cadence rewrites.
- Strong test performers pre-qualify for **Phase 2** (writing alternatives for the flagged-weak list).
- Screening signal: does their 2-sentence background name real business/editorial roles, and does their "what makes a phrase executive" answer show taste (status/register) vs grammar-focus?
