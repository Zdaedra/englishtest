Privacy Policy

Executive English · Effective 19 June 2026

Executive English (“we”, “the app”) helps you learn spoken English for real professional situations. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We keep data collection to the minimum the app needs to work.

1. What we collect

  • Account — your email address, an optional display name, and a securely hashed password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Learning data — your progress through lessons, practice scores, the text transcripts of your spoken answers, timestamps, and self-assessments. This is tied to your account so your progress and review schedule work across sessions.
  • Subscription status — if you subscribe, Apple tells us which plan is active and when it renews/expires. We never receive your card or payment details — Apple handles all payment.
  • Basic technical data — your interface-language choice and minimal request information needed to operate and secure the service.

2. Microphone & audio

When you speak an answer, the app records a short audio clip and sends it for transcription so we can score what you said. We do not store the audio — only the resulting text transcript is kept with your learning data. The microphone is used solely for the speaking exercises you start.

3. Service providers we use

  • OpenAI — to transcribe your speech, to evaluate and coach your answers, and to generate the spoken audio (text-to-speech) you hear. Audio and text needed for these tasks are processed by OpenAI on our behalf.
  • Apple — to process subscription payments (In-App Purchase) and, if you enable it, to deliver review reminders as local notifications.
  • Our hosting — your account and learning data are stored on our own server infrastructure.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. Why we’re allowed to use it

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (your account and learning), to keep it secure, and — where required — with your consent (e.g. microphone access, notifications). You can withdraw consent at any time in your device settings.

5. Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the app (Profile → Delete account), which permanently removes your account and associated learning data. You may also contact us to request deletion.

6. Children

Executive English is intended for adult professional learners and is not directed to children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We’ll revise the “Effective” date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: support@executive-english.net
