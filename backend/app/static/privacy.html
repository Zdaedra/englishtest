Privacy Policy

Executive English · Effective 23 June 2026

Jump to: EveryoneU.S. / CaliforniaEU / UKSubprocessors

Executive English (“we”, “the app”) helps you learn spoken English for real professional situations. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, who processes it, and the choices and rights you have. We keep data collection to the minimum the app needs to work, and we do not use advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

1. What we collect

  • Account — your email address, an optional display name, your interface-language choice, and a securely hashed password (never stored in readable form).
  • Learning data — your progress, practice scores, spaced-repetition state, timestamps, and the text transcripts of your spoken answers, tied to your account so your progress works across sessions.
  • Voice — when you speak an answer, a short audio clip is captured and sent for transcription so we can score what you said. We do not store the audio — only the text transcript is kept. Voice is used only for speech-to-text scoring; we do not use it to identify you and we do not create a voiceprint.
  • Subscription status — if you subscribe, Apple tells us which plan is active and when it renews/expires, plus a transaction identifier. We never receive your card or payment details — Apple handles all payment.
  • Profile photo — optional; stored only on your device and not uploaded to us.

3. Who processes your data (subprocessors)

We share data only with vendors that process it on our behalf under contracts that forbid using it for their own purposes or to train their models. See the full subprocessor list below. In short: OpenAI (speech-to-text and answer scoring/coaching), Anthropic (answer coaching/content generation), Resend (verification email), Hetzner (hosting, in Germany), and Apple (App Store purchases and, if enabled, local notifications). We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. International data

Your account and learning data are stored on a server in Germany (EU). To transcribe and score your answers, transcripts are processed by OpenAI and Anthropic in the United States, and verification email by Resend (U.S.). These transfers are covered by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum), which require the recipient to protect your data. You can request a copy of the safeguards at the contact below.

6. Your choices & rights

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account), which permanently removes your account and learning data, and you can export a copy of your data from the app. You can turn voice features and reminders on or off at any time. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights — see the U.S./California and EU/UK sections. To exercise any right, contact us at the address below; we respond within the time the law requires.

7. Children

Executive English is intended for adult professional learners and is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes & contact

We may update this policy; we’ll revise the “Effective” date and, for material changes, notify you in the app. Questions or requests: support@executive-english.net.

9. U.S. & California residents

This section provides the disclosures required by the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) and serves as our Notice at Collection.

Categories collected

  • Identifiers (email, name, account/transaction id)
  • Commercial information (subscription plan)
  • Audio/electronic information (voice and transcripts of your spoken answers)
  • Internet/activity (your learning interactions and progress)
  • Inferences (your skill/mastery state)

We collect these directly from you (and a purchase confirmation from Apple) to provide the service described above. We do not sell or share your personal information (including no “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising), and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months.

Sensitive personal information

Account credentials, and the content of your spoken answers, may be considered sensitive. We use them only to provide the service (authentication and scoring) — never for purposes that would trigger a right to limit — so no “Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information” action is needed.

Your California rights

You have the right to know/access, delete, and correct your personal information, to opt out of any sale/sharing (we do neither), and to non-discrimination for exercising your rights. Use in-app delete and export, or contact us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any right.

10. EU / UK residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

We are the data controller for your in-app data. Our legal bases are listed in section 2. You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability, and to withdraw consent (e.g. voice, reminders) at any time without affecting prior lawful processing. Self-service export and delete are in the app; for anything else, contact us and we’ll respond within one month. International transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (section 4). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in the UK, the ICO).
