Support

Executive English

Write to support@executive-english.net and we will get back to you. Please include the email address of your account and, if something went wrong, what you were doing when it happened.

Your subscription

Seeing, changing or cancelling it

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, so they are managed in your Apple account: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. There you can switch between plans, cancel, or see the next renewal date. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

I paid but the app still shows the free plan

Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore purchases. The app asks the App Store what you own and then re-checks it with our server. If it still does not appear, write to us with the date of the purchase and we will look it up.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Once Apple approves a refund, your plan in the app returns to free automatically.

What the plans include

  • Free — one complete topic, including the spoken practice and AI scoring, so you can try the whole method before paying.
  • Core — the full library, the learning path and the swipe practice, with audio.
  • AI — everything in Core plus the microphone: you speak your answer and it is scored and coached.

Your account

I forgot my password

There is no self-service password reset in the app yet. Write to support@executive-english.net from the email address you signed up with and we will help you back in. Your learning progress is stored on our server and is not lost.

I did not get the confirmation email

Check the spam folder first. The app can send the link again: the screen that asks you to confirm has a resend button. If it still does not arrive, write to us.

Changing your email

In the app, open your profile and change the address there. The new address starts unconfirmed and a fresh confirmation link is sent to it.

Deleting your account

In the app, open your profile and choose to delete the account. This erases your account, your learning progress and every stored transcript of your speech, and it cannot be undone. Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel that separately in your Apple account settings, as described above.

Microphone and audio

The microphone does not work

iOS asks for permission the first time you record. If you declined it, open Settings → Executive English on your iPhone and turn the microphone back on. Speech recognition has its own switch in the same place.

What happens to my voice

Your recording is sent for transcription and then discarded — the audio itself is never stored. The text of your answer is kept so the app can track your progress, and it is deleted automatically after 365 days, or immediately if you delete your account. We ask for your agreement on screen before the first recording. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Audio stops when I lock the screen

It should not: a practice session is rendered as one continuous audio file precisely so that the pauses survive a locked screen. If it does stop, tell us which iPhone and which iOS version you have.

Your data

You can ask us for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted, at any time, by writing to support@executive-english.net. Deleting your account in the app does the same thing immediately. What we collect and who processes it is set out in the Privacy Policy; the rules of use are in the Terms of Use.

Something is broken

If the app looks empty, a lesson will not open, or audio will not play, write to us with: the email of your account, what you tapped, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps. We read every message.

Executive English is made by a small team. We answer in English and in Russian.
